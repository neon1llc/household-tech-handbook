As household tech lead, you're its single point of failure. Make it possible for someone else to step in.

## Legacy and recovery contacts

Each major platform lets you name someone who can access your account if you die or can't use it:

- **Apple:** Legacy Contact, and a separate Account Recovery Contact, in Sign-In & Security settings.
- **Google:** Inactive Account Manager, at myaccount.google.com/inactive.
- **Facebook:** a legacy contact. **Instagram:** memorialization only. Instagram's Help Center describes no legacy contact.
- **Password managers:** 1Password's emergency kit, Bitwarden's emergency access, and similar features.

Set these up for every adult, and help parents set theirs. If a menu name doesn't match, search the provider's help for "legacy contact" or "inactive account".

## The emergency card

Fill in the [emergency card](../templates/emergency-card.md). It points the family to email, the password manager, phone passcodes, the photos, and the subscriptions to cancel or keep. Store it with your will or other important papers, and tell a second adult. Talk it through again whenever something big changes.

## Do this

- [ ] Legacy contacts and password manager emergency access set.
- [ ] Emergency card filled in and stored with important papers.
- [ ] A second adult knows where everything is.
