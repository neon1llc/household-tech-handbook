---
title: "12. When something goes wrong"
description: "The first steps for the common household emergencies: a hacked email, a lost phone, a scam, a data breach notice, or a child in trouble online."
---
Work through the steps in order. Act early.

## A hacked email or social account

1. From a trusted device, sign in and change the password. If you can't, use the provider's account recovery right away.
2. Turn on two-step sign-in, and sign out all other sessions.
3. Check forwarding rules, recovery details, and connected apps. Attackers change these to keep access.
4. Warn contacts not to trust recent messages from the account.

Playbook: [my email or social media account was hacked](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go/blob/main/content/playbooks/hacked-email.md).

## A lost or stolen phone

1. Use Find My or Find My Device to locate and lock it, with a contact number on the screen.
2. If it's gone, erase it remotely.
3. Call your carrier to suspend the line, and ask for a SIM lock or PIN.
4. Change your email and banking passwords, and remove the phone from your accounts.

Playbook: [my phone or laptop is lost or stolen](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go/blob/main/content/playbooks/lost-or-stolen-phone.md).

## Someone fell for a scam

1. Call the bank or card company right away, on the number on the card.
2. Change any password that was shared, and turn on two-step sign-in.
3. Report it at **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**, and see the [who-to-call card in Phishing Awareness Kit](https://github.com/neon1llc/phishing-awareness-kit/blob/main/handouts/who-to-call.md).
4. Stay blame-free, so the next one gets reported quickly too.

Playbook: [I think I got scammed](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go/blob/main/content/playbooks/scam-victim.md).

## A data breach notice arrived

Change the affected password, and anywhere else it was reused. Turn on two-step sign-in. If sensitive details such as a Social Security number were exposed, freeze your credit ([chapter 9](09-money-and-identity.md)).

Playbook: [I got a data breach notice](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go/blob/main/content/playbooks/data-breach-notice.md).

## A child is in trouble online

Thank them for telling you. Keep the evidence, block the account, get help before paying, and report it to the platform. If images of a minor are threatened, report to the NCMEC CyberTipline and use Take It Down.

Playbook: [my child is in trouble online](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go/blob/main/content/playbooks/kid-in-trouble-online.md).

## Afterward

Note what happened and what you changed on the household tech sheet. Then do the [yearly checkup](../checkups/yearly-checkup.md) early, since one problem often points to another.

## Sources

- [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/), from the FTC: where to report a scam.
- [Sextortion](https://www.missingkids.org/sextortion), from the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children: get help before paying, keep the messages, block, and report.
- [Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/), from NCMEC: help removing nude or explicit images of minors from participating platforms.
