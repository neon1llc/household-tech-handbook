---
title: "7. Helping aging parents"
description: "Make a parent's devices simpler and safer, set up remote help, and protect them from scams, without taking away their independence."
---
Aim for a setup your parent can manage alone, with you as a backup they trust. The [visiting parents checklist](../checkups/visiting-parents.md) covers the setup below in about two hours. Do it in person the first time.

## Simplify

- **Fewer apps** on the home screen, larger and bolder text.
- **Accessibility features:** Live Captions, hearing aid pairing, Magnifier, and voice control.
- **A password manager** they can use, such as their phone's built-in one, and the two-step method they find easiest.
- **Automatic updates.**

## Set up remote help

- **Screen sharing** tested together: FaceTime, Google Meet, or a remote support tool you trust. Agree that only you ever connect.
- **Find My** location sharing with you, if they agree.
- **Your number** in their favorites.

## Guard against scams

Grandparent and tech support scams target older adults.

- **Silence unknown callers** or turn on call screening, with family and doctors saved as contacts.
- **Bank alerts** for large withdrawals, sent to them and, with permission, to you. Many banks also let you name a trusted contact.
- **A credit freeze** at all three bureaus ([chapter 9](09-money-and-identity.md)).
- **A family code word** for emergency calls, and the habit of hanging up and calling back.
- **"Call me first"** before any request for money, gift cards, or remote access.

[Phishing Awareness Kit](https://github.com/neon1llc/phishing-awareness-kit) walks through the common scams and has a one-page takeaway to leave by the phone.

## Plan ahead

Settle what happens if they're ill or can't manage their accounts: a legacy contact, a list of accounts, where they're kept, and who has authority ([chapter 11](11-emergencies-and-legacy.md)).

## Do this

- [ ] Devices simplified and remote help tested.
- [ ] Unknown callers screened, bank alerts and a credit freeze in place.
- [ ] A code word and "call me first" agreed.
