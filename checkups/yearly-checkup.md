---
title: Yearly checkup
description: "One afternoon a year to keep the household's tech in shape: accounts, backups, the router, devices, money, and emergency plans."
---
Keep the [household tech sheet](../templates/household-tech-sheet.md) open and update it as you go.

## Accounts

- [ ] Two-step sign-in still on for every person's email and bank.
- [ ] Recovery details checked on each [account recovery sheet](../templates/account-recovery-sheet.md).
- [ ] Password manager's breach and reuse report reviewed, flagged passwords changed.
- [ ] People who've moved out removed from shared vaults and family groups.

## Backups, router, and devices

- [ ] Backups ran recently, and cloud storage isn't full.
- [ ] A restore tested, with the date recorded.
- [ ] Router firmware current. If it no longer gets updates, plan a replacement.
- [ ] Devices on the network match the inventory. Remove anything unknown.
- [ ] Every device has updates, screen lock, encryption, and find my device on.
- [ ] Devices that stopped getting updates retired ([chapter 10](../handbook/10-buying-and-retiring.md)).
- [ ] [Device inventory](../templates/device-inventory.csv) up to date.

## Kids, money, and emergencies

- [ ] Kids' controls adjusted for age, and the family tech talk had again.
- [ ] Credit reports checked at AnnualCreditReport.com for every adult.
- [ ] Credit freezes still in place, including for children.
- [ ] Legacy contacts and password manager emergency access still correct.
- [ ] [Emergency card](../templates/emergency-card.md) current, and a second adult knows where it is.
- [ ] Yearly [visiting parents](visiting-parents.md) checkup booked.

## Sources

- [Free credit reports](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/free-credit-reports), from the FTC: weekly free reports from each bureau at AnnualCreditReport.com.
- [What to know about credit freezes and fraud alerts](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts), from the FTC: freezes are free, don't affect your score, and can be placed for children under 16.
