---
title: Yearly checkup
description: "One afternoon a year to keep the household's tech in shape."
---
Keep the [household tech sheet](../templates/household-tech-sheet.md) open and update it as you go.

## Accounts

- [ ] Two-step sign-in still on for every person's email and bank.
- [ ] Each [account recovery sheet](../templates/account-recovery-sheet.md) checked.
- [ ] Password manager breach and reuse report reviewed, flagged passwords changed.
- [ ] People who've moved out removed from shared vaults and family groups.

## Backups, router, and devices

- [ ] Backups ran recently, and cloud storage isn't full.
- [ ] A restore tested, date recorded.
- [ ] Router firmware current. If it no longer gets updates, plan a replacement.
- [ ] Devices on the network match the [device inventory](../templates/device-inventory.csv). Remove anything unknown.
- [ ] Updates, screen lock, encryption, and find my device on everywhere.
- [ ] Devices that stopped getting updates retired ([chapter 10](../handbook/10-buying-and-retiring.md)).

## Kids, money, and emergencies

- [ ] Kids' controls adjusted for age, and the family tech talk had again.
- [ ] Credit reports checked at AnnualCreditReport.com for every adult.
- [ ] Credit freezes still in place, including for children.
- [ ] Legacy contacts and password manager emergency access still correct.
- [ ] [Emergency card](../templates/emergency-card.md) current, and a second adult knows where it is.
- [ ] [Visiting parents](visiting-parents.md) checkup booked.
